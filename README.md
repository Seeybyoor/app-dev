# My Favorite Series: Squid Game

**Squid Game** is a Korean series about people joining deadly games to win money.  
It shows how far people can go when they are desperate.

## Main Characters
1. Seong Gi-hun (Player 456)  
2. Cho Sang-woo (Player 218)  
3. Kang Sae-byeok (Player 067)  
4. Abdul Ali (Player 199)

## Why I Like It
- The story is exciting  
- The characters are emotional  
- It has a good lesson about life and choices  

> "You can’t call it a fair world when you’re only the one getting hurt."
